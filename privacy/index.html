Privacy policy

VDO Factory is a private, single-operator tool. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own original short videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts as drafts.

What it accesses

  • Your TikTok account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) — to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload). You review, edit and publish every draft yourself in the TikTok app.

What it stores, and where

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner's user account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Removing access

Disconnect the account in the app (Settings › Social channels › Disconnect), or revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.
